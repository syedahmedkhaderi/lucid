# Vendor Renewal Review Policy

**Fictional evaluation document — all organizations and figures are synthetic.**
**Review period:** Q4 2026
**Purpose:** Give the procurement manager and account owner a consistent, evidence-based renewal decision.

## Risk score bands

- 0–19: Low
- 20–39: Moderate
- 40–69: High
- 70–100: Critical

The 0–100 supplier risk score combines delivery reliability, service-level breaches, and overdue balance. A high score is a review trigger; it is not, by itself, an approval or termination decision.

## Renewal recovery gate

Do not approve a renewal while a supplier is on a recovery plan. At the 30-day review, all of the following must be true:

1. On-time delivery is at least 95%.
2. The balance overdue by more than 30 days is zero.
3. There has been no more than one SLA breach in the rolling 30-day period.
4. Finance has reconciled the balance and the Procurement Operations manager has signed off in writing.

The account owner reviews route variance and the four measures weekly. If any condition fails at day 30, keep renewal on hold and agree the next corrective action with the supplier.

## Terms used in the vendor register

- **Open balance** is the total unpaid amount.
- **Overdue >30 days** is the part of the open balance whose invoice is more than 30 days past due. It is a subset of open balance, not an additional amount.
- **On-time delivery** is the percent of deliveries arriving within the contracted delivery window during the review period.
- **SLA breaches** are recorded failures against a contracted service-level measure during the rolling 30-day period.
- **Current recommendation** is the account team's recommendation. It is not final approval.
